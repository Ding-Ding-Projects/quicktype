# App logo

Choose one of four shipped logo colourways or upload your own PNG, JPEG or WebP image, which is centre-cropped to a square.

## How to use it

Settings, Appearance, App logo.

## When something goes wrong

Files over 2 MiB, files whose bytes are not really an allowed image, and images that cannot be decoded safely are refused and the current logo stays.

## Privacy and security

The image is processed on your device and stored locally. It is never uploaded. It changes the picture only, never the installed app identity.

## How to check it works

Upload, restart, replace and reset.
