# Notifications

Messages appear as short toasts that never block what you are doing, and stay in the notification centre behind the bell until you clear them. Errors stay longer and are announced to screen readers immediately.

## How to use it

Settings, Language and wording, Show emoji in dialogs adds a decorative emoji; buttons and labels never get one.

## When something goes wrong

Notifications are kept for the current session only.

## Privacy and security

Nothing is sent anywhere.

## How to check it works

Trigger a success and an error and check the centre lists both.
