# Two-key confirmation

Anything that deletes data asks you to type a word and then press and hold the confirm button. Either step alone does nothing.

## How to use it

The dialog names exactly what will be affected. Esc or Cancel backs out.

## When something goes wrong

Releasing the button early cancels the hold.

## Privacy and security

This protects against slips, not against someone with access to your account.

## How to check it works

Try to delete history with only one of the two steps.
