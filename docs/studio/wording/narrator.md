# Narrator and sounds

An optional spoken narrator reads app events aloud in English, Cantonese or both (English first, then Cantonese), and short synthesized sounds mark actions. The narrator is off until you turn it on; sounds are on and can be turned off.

## How to use it

Settings, Narrator and sounds: choose a voice for each language from the voices installed on your device (or Choose automatically), and set rate and pitch. Test plays a sample.

## When something goes wrong

If your chosen voice is not installed, the narrator says it is using another voice and keeps your choice. If no voice can read a language, the setting says so. Lines are spaced out and never overlap; errors are always spoken.

## Privacy and security

Speech uses your system's voices. Voices marked online may need the network.

## How to check it works

Pick each voice, press Test, then uninstall the voice and check the fallback message.
