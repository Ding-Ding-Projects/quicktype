# English, Cantonese and bilingual

The whole interface is available in English, Hong Kong Cantonese, or both at once. In bilingual mode English comes first with Cantonese underneath in smaller type.

## How to use it

Settings, Language and wording, Display language. The choice applies immediately and is remembered.

## When something goes wrong

Text that has no Cantonese translation falls back to English rather than showing a key.

## Privacy and security

Language choice is a local setting.

## How to check it works

Switch between the three modes on every tab and check nothing is cut off at narrow widths.
